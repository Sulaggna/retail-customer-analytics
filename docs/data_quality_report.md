## Notes

- Final table: **1,033,036 rows**, of which **1,003,340 sale lines** across **39,516 invoices** and **5,852 identified customers**.
- 6,179 of 17,914 cancellation lines (34%) were matched to the exact sale they reversed; median time from sale to cancellation was 263 hours. Unmatched cancellations are partial returns or reverse sales made before the data window.
- Customer IDs are missing on ~23% of raw rows. These look like guest / manual orders: they are kept in revenue KPIs and excluded from anything customer-level (cohorts, RFM, CLV).
- The two largest single lines in the data (80,995 and 74,215 units) were both cancelled within minutes. They are kept and netted out by their cancellations rather than deleted by hand.
- 1,200+ stock codes have more than one description; the most frequent description on sale lines is used as the canonical product name.
